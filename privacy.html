Privacy Policy

 Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Welcome to QuickChart. This Privacy Policy describes how QuickChart ("we," "our," or "us") handles information. We have designed this Application with a privacy-first approach specifically for the sensitive nature of EMS documentation.

1. Information Collection

QuickChart does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) or Protected Health Information (PHI). Narratives, facility lists, patient data, and anything else you type into the Application stay on your device. The only information you enter that is ever sent to us is the text of a bug report you choose to submit. The Application also sends limited, anonymous usage information (see Section 5).

2. How Your Information is Processed

All processing of narratives, facility lists, and patient data occurs locally within your browser. Because we do not receive your data, we cannot use, see, sell, or share it with any third parties.

3. Local Browser Storage

The Application utilizes local browser storage (such as localStorage or IndexedDB) to save your settings and documentation drafts.

  • Control: Your drafts and settings never leave your device.
  • Deletion: Clearing your browser cache or "Site Data" will permanently delete any stored information.
  • Security: You are responsible for maintaining the physical and digital security of your device to prevent unauthorized access to this local storage.

4. HIPAA and Regulatory Compliance

Because QuickChart does not "create, receive, maintain, or transmit" PHI on behalf of a covered entity (it merely provides a local tool for the user to do so), QuickChart does not function as a Business Associate as defined by HIPAA. Users are responsible for ensuring their use of the Application aligns with their employer's specific data privacy and retention policies.

5. Usage Information, Bug Reports, and Third Parties

This Application does not contain third-party trackers, cookies, or advertisements, and does not fingerprint your device. Any links provided within the app are for user convenience only.

Anonymous Usage Information: To understand how widely QuickChart is used, the Application may store a random identifier in your browser and send it to us with basic, non-identifying usage information (such as which template was used and the Application version). It never includes anything you type, and it is not linked to your identity. Clearing your browser’s site data resets the identifier.

Bug Report Tool: If you choose to submit a bug report, its text and basic technical details (such as the page and browser type) are sent to us over an encrypted connection. We, and service providers acting on our behalf, may use reports only to investigate and fix problems with the Application. Do not include patient information, PHI, or any other sensitive or identifying data in a bug report — describe only the technical issue you encountered.

6. Changes to This Policy

As the Software evolves, we may update this policy to reflect changes in how local data is handled. The "Effective Date" at the top will reflect the most recent version; please check it periodically. Continued use of the Application after a change means you accept the updated policy.
